# Imagegen 提示词

生成方式：内置 `image_gen`，用途为应用商店宣传图中的立体插画。标题、功能说明、品牌标识与界面示意由 SVG 绘制。

## 01-rotor-orbit.png

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: text-free central illustration for the existing Rotor Authenticator HarmonyOS app store campaign; final artwork will be composed in an editable SVG.
Primary request: A museum-quality minimal 3D editorial still life of one sculptural, open circular countdown orbit made of thick optical cobalt-blue glass, with a smaller milky frosted glass inner arc. It conveys rotating one-time authentication codes, elegant precision and simplicity.
Scene/backdrop: seamless extremely pale cool porcelain white (#F4F7FC) studio cyclorama, soft floor contact shadow, no horizon line. The color palette is the existing Rotor blue (#0A59F7), ice blue and white.
Composition/framing: square image, the orbit sits at a slight three-quarter angle and fills 75% of the canvas, centered. The upper left of the sculpture rises higher than the right. The center opening is broad and empty so a real vector verification-code card can be overlaid later. The complete sculpture is within the image with generous margin. A single delicate luminous arc follows its curvature. Subtle depth of field, crisp edges.
Materials/textures: polished translucent glass, soft caustics, pearlescent inner surface, precise smooth bevels. Sophisticated consumer software campaign, restrained and premium, not a game prop.
Lighting/mood: large softbox from upper left, gentle blue refractions on the ground, luminous daylight, refined visual hierarchy.
Text: none.
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numerals, no logos, no QR codes, no phones, no interface, no shields, no padlocks, no stars, no confetti, no watermarks. Do not invent application UI.
```
